Last Updated: April 2025

The Obrolan Hati application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server or third-party service. All data remains exclusively on the user's device.

Since all data is stored locally on the device, users can delete all data by uninstalling the application from their device.

8. Children's Privacy

This application is rated for users aged 13 and above and is not directed at children under the age of 13.
